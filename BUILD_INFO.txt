News retention patch:
- Keep dated news for the current simulation week and the immediately previous simulation week (Monday-Sunday).
- Prune old news on startup, before save, and at the SQLite persistence boundary.
- Rebuild news_items on every save; old rows cannot accumulate.
- Startup backfill only recreates current/previous-week match articles, so deleted historical news is not regenerated.
- The initial database has been pruned to the same two-week window and vacuumed.

Storage de-duplication patch:
- Completed archives are incrementally upserted; unchanged seasons are not rewritten.
- Match, finance transaction, and award SQL copies are no longer written; canonical data remains in career_state/season_archives.
- Exact duplicate player-career/development projection rows are removed once; unmatched legacy rows are preserved.
- player_season_stats is retained because its detailed per-season fields are not fully represented by compact archives.
- Existing package database was compacted and vacuumed after integrity and data-equivalence checks.

Preserved data:
- All 8 completed season archives (2026-2033).
- All 2,213 match records remain in their canonical locations: 1,968 in the eight completed-season archives and 245 in the active career state.
- Player profiles, season stats, development records, award history, finance ledger and transfers are preserved; only exact SQL duplicates are removed.
- 51 unmatched legacy player-career rows and 182 unmatched legacy player-development rows remain preserved in their supplemental tables.
- The only canonical career-state payload intentionally removed is news outside the two-week window.
